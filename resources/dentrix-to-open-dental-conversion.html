Switching From Dentrix to Open Dental: What Converts and What to Plan For

Moving from Dentrix to Open Dental? Here's what Open Dental says converts, what doesn't, how X-rays and documents are handled, and how to plan go-live week.

Published November 25, 20264-minute readPublished by Odyssey Solutions

Dentrix is one of the most common practice management systems in dental offices, and it's one of the systems Open Dental converts from most often. If you're planning the move, the conversion itself is the easy part. The work is knowing what comes across, saving what doesn't, and getting imaging and every workstation ready before go-live.

This guide covers Dentrix G-series (the version installed on your own server). If you're on Dentrix Ascend, Henry Schein One's cloud product, Open Dental lists it as a separate conversion source. Open Dental: Conversion list For the general plan, see how to switch dental practice management software.

What converts from Dentrix

According to Open Dental's Dentrix conversion guide, these come across: Open Dental: Dentrix conversion guide

  • Patients and family relationships
  • Insurance plans
  • Primary claims
  • Payments and adjustments
  • Payment plans (the remaining balance)
  • Clinical notes and perio charts
  • Prescriptions, medication and disease lists
  • Appointments
  • Treatment plans
  • Referrals

What doesn't convert

The same guide lists what stays behind:

  • X-rays (handled separately, below)
  • Secondary claims
  • Preauthorizations
  • Forms and questionnaires
  • Auto notes
  • Custom recall intervals

How to handle them:

  1. Before the cutoff, run an outstanding claims report in Dentrix, including secondaries and preauthorizations, and work those from the report.
  2. Write down your custom recall intervals and auto notes so they can be rebuilt in Open Dental.
  3. Rebuild patient forms in Open Dental's own forms.
  4. Run and save the historical reports your office and accountant use. Open Dental's guide says to run historical reports in Dentrix before converting.

Treatment plans need one extra file

Open Dental's guide notes that converting Dentrix treatment plans needs a specific Dentrix file (txcase.dat). Make sure whoever pulls your data knows to include it, and check a few treatment plans in the test conversion. Open Dental: Dentrix conversion guide

X-rays and scanned documents

  • X-rays: if your X-rays are in a separate imaging program, such as Dexis or Carestream, you can usually keep it and bridge it to Open Dental. Open Dental says images from these programs line up close to 100% if they were used in bridged mode with Dentrix. Open Dental: Image conversions If they weren't, budget for an image conversion.
  • Scanned documents: Open Dental's Dentrix guide says you need to ask Dentrix for a decrypted backup to convert scanned documents. Request it early, because it isn't instant.

The conversion steps

  1. Free test conversion. Open Dental's team connects to your Dentrix server and pulls the data. For a supported system like Dentrix, the test takes a few hours. Open Dental: Test conversion
  2. Review the test with Open Dental's pre-conversion checklist. Compare at least 10 patients with balances and insurance, and your total accounts receivable. Pre-conversion checklist
  3. Book the final conversion one to two weeks ahead and stop entering data in Dentrix at the cutoff. Open Dental: Final conversion
  4. Final conversion, then the optional image conversion.
  5. Setup and training. Open Dental offers a free post-conversion setup session if it's done within 30 days, plus remote training. Open Dental: Training

Cost (as listed in November 2026)

Open Dental lists Dentrix data conversion at $1,450, with scanned documents and X-rays as separate conversions. Open Dental: Conversion list Extra clinics are $400 each. Open Dental: Conversions Check the current list before budgeting.

The IT side

Both Dentrix G-series and Open Dental usually run on a server in your office, so you can often reuse the server if it meets Open Dental's requirements. Plan for:

  • Installing Open Dental and the imaging bridge on every workstation and testing every sensor and camera
  • Moving or reusing the shared image folder
  • Backups for the new database, tested with a restore
  • Keeping Dentrix available read-only for lookups, since Texas requires dental records to be kept at least five years after the last visit 22 Tex. Admin. Code § 108.8

FAQ

Do my Dentrix X-rays transfer to Open Dental?

Not with the data conversion. If they're in separate imaging software, you can usually bridge it to Open Dental. Otherwise, they need a separate image conversion.

Do Dentrix insurance claims transfer?

Primary claims do, according to Open Dental. Secondary claims and preauthorizations don't, so work them from a report.

How long does a Dentrix to Open Dental conversion take?

The test conversion takes a few hours. Plan several weeks from test to go-live, and a lighter schedule for the first week or two.
