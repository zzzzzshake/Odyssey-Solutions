Switching From Denticon to Open Dental: Getting Your Data Out of the Cloud

Leaving Denticon for Open Dental? Your data lives on Planet DDS's servers, so the first job is getting a copy. Here's what converts and how to plan the move.

Published November 30, 20264-minute readPublished by Odyssey Solutions

Denticon, from Planet DDS, is cloud software: your schedule, ledgers and charts live on Planet DDS's servers, not in your office. That changes the order of a conversion. With an office server, the new vendor can connect and pull a copy. With Denticon, you have to get the data out first, and you have to do it before your contract ends.

This guide covers that, plus what converts to Open Dental and the IT you'll need, since Open Dental usually runs on a server in your office. For the general plan, see how to switch dental practice management software.

Step one: read your Denticon agreement

Planet DDS agreements published on its website make getting your data out the practice's responsibility. Both the 2021 and 2022 versions say:

  • Extracting your data before termination is the client's sole responsibility.
  • You can ask, within 10 business days after termination, for your data to be placed on a secure site for 30 days, and Planet DDS may charge a reasonable fee.
  • Your data is deleted 30 to 90 days after termination.

Planet DDS: Denticon sales agreement (July 2022), Planet DDS: SaaS agreement (October 2021)

Your contract may be newer or different, so check the one you signed. The point stands: request your data before you cancel, not after, and ask Planet DDS in writing what format you'll get, what it includes and what it costs.

What converts from Denticon

Open Dental lists Denticon (versions from 2009 through 2024) as a conversion source. Its list shows progress notes, perio and prescriptions converting, and medical history coming across as a clinical note. Insurance plans may need some cleanup after conversion. Open Dental: Conversion list

As with any conversion, claims generally don't convert, so plan to work outstanding claims from a report. Pre-conversion checklist

Before you lose access to Denticon, save:

  • Outstanding claims and preauthorizations
  • Aging, production and collections reports
  • Any report your accountant or partners rely on
  • A list of recall settings and templates to rebuild

X-rays and documents

Open Dental says Denticon images have to be downloaded from Denticon's FTP site after Denticon grants access. Open Dental: Image conversions Ask for image access at the same time as the data request, and make sure there's enough storage on your new server for years of images.

The conversion steps

  1. Request your data from Planet DDS and confirm the format and timing.
  2. Free test conversion. For a cloud system, Open Dental's checklist says the practice supplies a recent backup instead of server access. Pre-conversion checklist, Open Dental: Test conversion
  3. Review the test: at least 10 patients with balances and insurance, your total accounts receivable and insurance plans.
  4. Get a fresh export at the cutoff for the final conversion, and stop entering data in Denticon. Open Dental: Final conversion
  5. Final conversion, image conversion, setup and training.

Cost (as listed in November 2026)

Open Dental lists Denticon data conversion at $1,450. Open Dental: Conversion list Add any fee Planet DDS charges for the export, and image conversion if needed.

The IT side: you're moving from cloud to your own server

Coming from Denticon, many offices don't have a server at all. Open Dental normally runs on a server in the office. Open Dental also offers a hosted Open Dental Cloud, listed as a limited-release option for US customers with its own monthly fee. Open Dental: Open Dental Cloud

If you go with a server in the office, you'll need:

  • A server sized for the database and years of images
  • A reliable office network and every workstation set up for Open Dental
  • Imaging capture software and bridges in every operatory
  • Backups, tested with a restore, with a copy kept off-site
  • Secure remote access for owners and billing staff who used to log in from anywhere

That's a real change from cloud software, and it's the part we help practices plan. Our Open Dental conversion IT checklist has the full list.

Records you still have to keep

Texas requires dental records to be kept at least five years after the last treatment, and longer for minors. 22 Tex. Admin. Code § 108.8 Since Denticon's data is deleted after termination, make sure everything you're required to keep is either converted or saved somewhere you control.

FAQ

Can Open Dental pull my data straight from Denticon?

No. Denticon is cloud software, so you get a copy from Planet DDS and give it to Open Dental for conversion.

When should I cancel Denticon?

After you have your data and images, and after the final conversion is checked. Check your contract's notice period and data deletion terms first.

Do I need a server for Open Dental?

Usually, yes, unless you choose Open Dental Cloud. Plan the server, backups and remote access before go-live.
