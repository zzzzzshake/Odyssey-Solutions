Excel Showing Formulas or #### Instead of Numbers? How to Fix Both

If Excel shows formulas instead of answers, or #### instead of a number or date, here are the quick fixes, plus what to check when the quick fix doesn't work.

Published November 23, 20264-minute readPublished by Odyssey Solutions

You open a spreadsheet and the totals are gone. Either every cell shows its formula, like =SUM(B2:B14), or a column is full of #### where the numbers and dates should be. Nothing is broken in either case. Both are display problems, and both usually take a few seconds to fix.

This guide covers the quick fix for each one and what to check when the quick fix doesn't work.

Problem 1: Excel shows formulas instead of answers

The quick fix: press Ctrl and the grave accent key

Press Ctrl + ` (the grave accent key, usually just left of the 1 key at the top of the keyboard). Excel switches between showing formulas and showing results. You can also go to the Formulas tab and select Show Formulas to turn it off. Microsoft: Display or hide formulas

This setting usually gets turned on by accident, because the shortcut is easy to hit. When it's on, Excel also widens the columns to fit the formulas, and puts them back when you switch it off. Microsoft: How to avoid broken formulas

If only one cell shows its formula

If Show Formulas is off but one cell still shows the formula as text, the cell is probably formatted as Text. Microsoft's fix: right-click the cell, choose Format Cells, pick General, then press F2 and Enter so Excel reads the formula again. Microsoft: How to avoid broken formulas

This happens a lot in files that started as an export from another system or a CSV. Our guide to Excel and CSV leading zeros covers the other side of that problem, when you want Excel to treat something as text.

If the formula is gone completely

Show Formulas only changes what you see. If a formula was replaced by a plain number, for example when someone saved the file as a CSV, this setting won't bring it back. Check the file's version history, or ask whoever sent the file for the original.

Problem 2: Excel shows #### instead of a number or date

The quick fix: widen the column

means the column isn't wide enough to show the number or date in that cell. [Microsoft: How to correct a ##### error](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/how-to-correct-a-error)

  • Fastest: double-click the line on the right edge of the column letter at the top. Excel resizes the column to fit.
  • From the ribbon: select the column, go to Home, Format, then AutoFit Column Width. Microsoft: Change the column width and row height
  • Whole sheet at once: select the whole sheet, then double-click any line between two column letters.

If you can't make the column wider

Microsoft suggests changing the format instead. A long date like "Wednesday, November 25, 2026" can be shown as 11/25/26, and a number can be shown with fewer decimal places. Microsoft: How to correct a ##### error

If #### stays no matter how wide the column is

A date or time that works out to a negative value can show as #### at any width. That usually means a formula is subtracting dates in the wrong order, or a cell has the wrong date in it. Check the formula, or switch the cell to General format to see the actual value. Microsoft: How to correct a ##### error

When it's more than a display problem

Call your IT help if:

  • The file opens read-only or says it's locked by another user. Our Excel file locked for editing guide covers that one.
  • Formulas show wrong totals, not just the wrong display.
  • The same files act strangely for several people, which can point to a sync or version problem with the shared folder.

FAQ

Why is Excel showing the formula instead of the result?

Usually Show Formulas was turned on, often by pressing Ctrl + ` by accident. Press Ctrl + ` again or turn off Show Formulas on the Formulas tab. If only one cell does it, the cell is probably formatted as Text.

What does #### mean in Excel?

It means the column is too narrow to show the number or date in that cell. Double-click the right edge of the column header to fit it.

Why does a date show #### even when the column is wide?

The date or time is probably negative, often from a subtraction done in the wrong order. Check the formula or the dates it uses.

Does Show Formulas change my file?

No. It only changes what you see on screen. Your formulas and values stay the same.
